# Astro-AEO 1.5.3 semantic validation evidence

1.5.3 adds eight JSON-LD components and extends Article publishing props, so the 1.5.2 sign-off
is not carried forward. Both the rebuilt legacy fixture and the schema-tools fixture were submitted
as complete rendered HTML to Schema.org and Google's Rich Results Test using their code-input UIs.
No public deployment was needed. The schema-tools fixture exercises minimal, deliberately
incomplete entities and safe serialization; it is not a merchant-listing eligibility example.
The completed follow-up edits are included through `e694ab7`. Both fixtures were rebuilt at that
candidate, and their HTML hashes still match the exact files submitted earlier on 2026-10-02.
The rebuilt inputs preserve every submitted byte, not merely the JSON-LD values. The release owner
accepted the recorded results, the byte-identical carry-forward basis and the disclosed merchant
fixture limitations on 2026-10-02. No second external submission is needed for byte-identical input.

Schema-tools valid detections: Product snippets (2), Articles (4), Breadcrumbs (1), Datasets (1),
Local businesses (1), Organization (1), Profile page (1), Review snippets (2), and Software Apps (1).
Google reports non-critical issues for Product snippets, Articles, Datasets, Local businesses,
Organization and Software Apps. It reports no separate ItemList or Service result category.
Schema.org accepts those entity types; Google detection is a separate check.

The [two merchant-listing failures](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results/result/r%2Fmerchant-listings?id=V5-hil0y92lHcvBnwkzf9g)
are `Reviewed tool` and the serialization-test Product with a literal script-tag string in its name.
Each omits `image` and `offers.priceCurrency`, which Google reports as two critical issues. Both
fields are absent in the authored fixture; the components do not invent them. Both Products are valid
for the product-snippet profile. Optional merchant issues are description, global identifier,
availability, merchant return policy and shipping details. Acceptance of these known fixture
limitations does not claim merchant eligibility or full parity between the package's product-snippet
checker and Google's merchant-listing checker.

The safe-serialization Product name remains data in the submitted page. The static and SSR contract
tests verify escaped script delimiters and Unicode separators, preserved inputs, omitted
missing-entity output and empty authors, and unchanged legacy Article output. The local checker also
reports its unsupported-profile explanations without suppressing JSON-LD. These local explanations
are not claims about what Google's live validator can recognize.
