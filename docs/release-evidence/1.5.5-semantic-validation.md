## Semantic evidence carry-forward

The full gate rebuilt `fixtures/golden-1.2` and `fixtures/schema-tools/static`. Their complete
rendered `dist/index.html` files have the exact SHA-256 values recorded for the inputs submitted to
external validators in [1.5.3's evidence](1.5.3-semantic-validation.md) and carried forward in
[1.5.4's](1.5.4-semantic-validation.md). No component or schema builder source changed. No new
external validator submission was performed or is claimed.

The recorded results therefore carry forward for those byte-identical inputs: Schema.org found
zero errors and warnings in both fixtures; Google's legacy result had 3 valid items, while the
schema-tools result had 14 valid profile detections and 2 invalid merchant listings. The latter
remain intentionally incomplete authored fixtures missing images and offer currencies. These
limitations and non-critical issues remain disclosed, not recast as a clean merchant result.
