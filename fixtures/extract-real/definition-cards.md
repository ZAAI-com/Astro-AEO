**Pins stop competing with the map**

Markers are redrawn smaller, or replaced by a coloured dot, before Google Maps paints them into the map canvas. Streets, water, parks and place names are untouched.

**The colour still tells you what it is**

Every dot takes its colour from the pin it replaces. A restaurant stays red, a saved place stays yellow, a custom list stays cyan.

**Nothing moves**

Markers keep their original dimensions, so anchor points and click targets do not shift. Taps land exactly where they did before.
